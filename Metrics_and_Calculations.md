# Metrics & Calculations

The following business metrics were developed using MySQL and Python (Pandas, SciPy, and Statsmodels) to support revenue, conversion funnel, product performance, and website optimization analysis.

| **Metric** | **Formula / Calculation** | **Business Purpose** |
|---|---|---|
| **Gross Revenue** | `Σ Order Revenue` | Calculates total revenue generated from all orders before refunds. |
| **Refund Amount** | `Σ Refund Amount of Refunded Items` | Calculates the total value of refunded items. |
| **Net Revenue** | `Gross Revenue − Refund Amount` | Measures revenue retained after refunds. |
| **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)** | `Σ Order COGS` | Calculates the total cost of products sold. |
| **Profit** | `Net Revenue − COGS + Refunded COGS` | Measures profit after refunds, adding back the cost of refunded items. |
| **Gross Orders** | `Total Number of Orders` | Counts all orders placed. |
| **Net Orders** | `Number of Orders where Refund Amount ≠ Order Revenue` | Counts orders that were not fully refunded. |
| **Gross AOV** | `Gross Revenue ÷ Gross Orders` | Measures average order value before refunds. |
| **Net AOV** | `Net Revenue ÷ Net Orders` | Measures average order value after refunds. |
| **Refund Rate (%)** | `(Refund Amount ÷ Gross Revenue) × 100` | Measures the share of revenue lost to refunds overall and by product, campaign, or landing page. |
| **Financial Year** | `Year − 1 for January and February; Year for March to December` | Groups dates into financial years that begin in March. |
| **Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth (%)** | `((Value in FY − Value in Prior FY) ÷ Value in Prior FY) × 100` | Measures growth in revenue, orders, traffic, or other metrics compared with the previous financial year. |
| **Website Traffic** | `Total Number of Sessions` | Counts the total number of website sessions. |
| **Conversion Rate (%)** | `(Gross Orders ÷ Website Traffic) × 100` | Measures the share of sessions that resulted in an order. |
| **Funnel Traffic** | `Number of Pageviews at Each Funnel Step` | Counts pageviews recorded at each funnel step (Landing, Products, Item, Cart, Shipping, Billing, Thank You). |
| **Funnel Step Conversion Rate (%)** | `(Traffic at Step ÷ Traffic at Previous Step) × 100` | Measures the share of traffic that progresses from one funnel step to the next. |
| **Funnel Drop-Off Rate (%)** | `100 − Funnel Step Conversion Rate` | Identifies the funnel steps where the largest share of traffic is lost. |
| **Billing-to-Purchase Conversion (%)** | `(Thank You Traffic ÷ Billing Traffic) × 100`; for the A/B test, `(Orders ÷ Billing Traffic) × 100` for each billing page | Measures how effectively the billing step converts into completed purchases. |
| **Landing-Page Conversion Rate (%)** | `(Number of Unique Orders ÷ Landing Page Traffic) × 100` | Measures the share of landing-page traffic that resulted in an order by campaign, device, and landing page. |
| **Product Net Revenue** | `Σ Item Price of Non-Refunded Items` | Calculates revenue from product items that were not refunded. |
| **Product Revenue Contribution (%)** | `(Product Net Revenue ÷ Total Net Revenue) × 100` | Measures each product's contribution to net revenue and indicates concentration risk. |
| **Product Refund Contribution (%)** | `(Product Refund Amount ÷ Total Refund Amount) × 100` | Measures each product's contribution to total refunds. |
| **Product Margin (%)** | `(Product Profit ÷ Product Net Revenue) × 100`, where `Product Profit = Σ (Item Price − Item COGS)` for non-refunded items | Measures the profitability of each product or product combination relative to net revenue. |
| **Daily Revenue per Product** | `Product Net Revenue ÷ Number of Days between First and Last Order` | Estimates average daily net revenue generated by a product. |
| **Daily Orders per Product** | `Product Net Order Items ÷ Number of Days between First and Last Order` | Estimates average daily order volume for a product. |
| **Order Type** | `Items Purchased = 1 → Single-Item; Items Purchased = 2 → Double-Item` | Separates single-item and double-item purchases for basket analysis. |
| **Product Combination** | `Orders grouped by Combined Order Price` | Identifies product combinations by their combined price and compares their revenue, margin, and refund rate. |
| **Product-Page-to-Purchase Conversion (%)** | `(Sessions with Completed Purchase ÷ Sessions Viewing Product Page) × 100` | Measures how often a product-page visit results in a purchase. |
| **Product Add-to-Cart Conversion (%)** | `(Sessions Reaching Cart ÷ Sessions Viewing Product Page) × 100` | Measures how often a product-page visit progresses to the cart. |
| **Average Add-to-Cart Conversion (Benchmark) (%)** | `Σ Product Add-to-Cart Conversion ÷ Number of Products` | Provides a benchmark for identifying products with below-average add-to-cart conversion. |
| **95% Confidence Interval for a Proportion** | `p ± 1.96 × √(p × (1 − p) ÷ n)` | Estimates the range in which the true conversion rate is likely to fall and shows how precise the estimate is. |
| **Two-Proportion Z-Test** | `z = (p₁ − p₂) ÷ √(p̂ × (1 − p̂) × (1/n₁ + 1/n₂))`, where `p̂` is the pooled proportion | Tests whether the conversion rates of two groups, such as `/billing` and `/billing-2`, differ significantly. |
| **Chi-Square Test of Independence** | `χ² = Σ ((Observed − Expected)² ÷ Expected)` | Tests whether add-to-cart outcomes differ between pairs of products. |
| **Bonferroni-Adjusted Significance Level** | `α ÷ Number of Pairwise Comparisons` | Controls false positives when several product pairs are tested at the same time. |
| **Modeled Funnel Progression** | `Modeled Cart Reached = Item Views × Target Add-to-Cart Rate`; each later step = `Modeled Previous Step × Step Conversion Rate`, rounded down to a whole number | Estimates completed purchases if add-to-cart conversion reaches the target while later steps stay unchanged. |
| **Modeled Gross Revenue** | `Modeled Completed Purchases × Primary Product Average Price` | Estimates gross revenue under the add-to-cart improvement scenario. |
| **Modeled Net Revenue** | `Modeled Gross Revenue − Actual Primary Refund Amount` | Estimates net revenue under the scenario while holding refund amounts constant. |
| **Potential Net Revenue Recovery** | `Recovery Scenario Net Revenue − Actual Net Revenue` | Estimates the additional net revenue from improving add-to-cart conversion. |
| **Potential Recovery (%)** | `(Potential Net Revenue Recovery ÷ Actual Net Revenue) × 100` | Expresses the modeled recovery as a share of actual net revenue. |

## Business Assumptions

- **Gross revenue** is the total of all revenue recorded across orders.
- **Net revenue** is gross revenue minus refund amount.
- **Gross orders** is the total number of orders.
- **Net orders** is the number of orders whose refund amount does not equal their revenue. An order that was entirely refunded is treated as a fully refunded order and excluded; orders with no refund or only a partial refund are included.
- **Net AOV** is net revenue per net order.
- All monetary values are in USD.
- The financial year begins in March, so January and February belong to the preceding financial year. The dataset runs from March 19, 2012 to March 19, 2015. FY2012 therefore starts on March 19, and FY2015 contains only the first 19 days of March 2015. The financial-year analysis covers FY2012–FY2014.
- Profit assumes that the cost of refunded items is recovered, so refunded COGS is added back to profit.
- Product-level net revenue, net orders, and margin are calculated at the order-item level using items that were not refunded. They differ from the order-level net orders defined above.
- Product combinations are identified by the combined price of the order, because each combination in the dataset has a distinct total price.
- Funnel steps are assigned from pageview URLs, and `/billing` and `/billing-2` both belong to the Billing step. Funnel traffic counts the pageviews recorded at each step, so a session that opens the same step more than once is counted more than once.
- Product-level add-to-cart conversion is measured at the session level. The cart page does not identify which product was added, so a session that views more than one product is counted under each product it viewed.
- The average add-to-cart conversion benchmark is the simple average of product-level rates and does not weight products by traffic volume.
- Landing-page analysis uses records from August 2, 2014 onward, when the three active landing pages (`/home`, `/lander-3`, and `/lander-5`) were in use.
- The first six months of a product start from its first recorded order. The last six months cover the six months before the last recorded date in the dataset.
- The revenue recovery scenario assumes a 55% add-to-cart conversion rate for products whose rate is below the benchmark, unchanged conversion rates at all later funnel steps, and constant refund amounts. Each modeled funnel step is rounded down to a whole number, and secondary-product revenue is held constant.
- The billing-page comparison uses a two-sided two-proportion z-test on the period when both `/billing` and `/billing-2` were available (September 10, 2012 to January 5, 2013).
- Pairwise product comparisons use the chi-square test with a Bonferroni-adjusted significance level based on six comparisons among four products.
- Metrics exclude marketing spend, shipping costs, and operating expenses because these data are unavailable in the dataset.
